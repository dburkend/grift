Close MyWhoosh, Zwift and the Wattbike Hub app on every device first. On the Next Gen Atom with dual Bluetooth, the last app to connect gets control; anything else connected can block or steal ERG.

Guided ERG verification (~75 s)

Pedal at a steady cadence (aim for 85–90 rpm) and hold it the whole time. The test sends 100 W → 200 W → 140 W. It passes only if the bike acknowledges every command and measured power tracks each target while your cadence stays roughly constant, which can only happen if resistance is actually changing.
